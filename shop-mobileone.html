With Virtual Assistant

  1. You get a dedicated Virtual Assistant line in the shop’s name. Because dispatch is 24/7, the desk is overflow: ring you first, then the desk if there is no pickup.

  3. The desk captures location, unit type (truck / trailer / RV), and that you do not tow. It does not diagnose brakes or quote a part.

Why this shop, specifically

A 24-hour “technician not an answering service” shop still needs overflow when the technician is the answering service and is already on a job.

  • 24-hour dispatch is the product. Overflow keeps the promise when you cannot pick up.
  • No towing is a filter. The desk can say that so the wrong caller does not burn your night.
  • Geography is huge. Priest River through Spokane through western Montana.
  • Parts-on-hand is the margin. You only use it if the call reaches you.
  • You confirm every job. No ETA, no part quote, no diagnosis from the desk.

What the desk will not do: final quotes, taking payment, contracts, arrival times, brake/cooling diagnosis, offering a tow, or messages off-script. Cancel anytime after the first 30 days.
