A Sagle Creek number vs a voicemail

Seniors and neighbors do not hunt a website. They dial 208-304-3883. If it rings out, they call the next name they were given.

What happens now

  1. A neighbor on Sagle Creek needs a plumbing check. You are on a yard job.
  2. Voicemail. A lot of the people your listing is written for will not leave one.
  3. The 10% first-project offer and the senior discount never get said, because nobody picked up.
