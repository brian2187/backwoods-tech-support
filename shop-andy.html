Today vs a desk that answers after 4

Solo shops lose the after-hours call while they are still on the last job — or already home in Samuels. “By appointment” only works if someone is there to make the appointment.

What happens now

  1. Caller dials 208-920-3269 at 5:40 p.m. You are loading tools or driving home.
  2. Voicemail, or they hang up. Sandpoint has other numbers.
  3. If they do leave a message, you hear it after dinner. The leak already called someone else.
  4. Weekend / evening “by appointment” work never gets on a calendar.

With AIBizSupport

  1. You get a dedicated AIBiz line in the shop’s name. You set the hours (site already says Mon–Fri 7–4).
  2. During those hours the dedicated line rings you first. You pick up → the call is yours. No pickup → the desk answers as Andy The Handyman.
  3. After hours and weekends, the desk answers. Your phone does not have to ring.
  4. A calendar request lands on a calendar you own. You confirm. No quote, no promise you did not make.
