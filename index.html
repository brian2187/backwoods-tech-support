How it works when a customer actually calls

No mystery sauce. Four steps, then you are back in charge.

1

They dial

Your number forwards after hours, or they use the dedicated line. Either way, a human-sounding desk picks up.

2

Four or five questions

What they need, where they are, when they need it, how hot it is. Same questions you would ask if you were not covered in drywall dust.

3

Calendar + ping

If it is a real job, it becomes an event on your calendar and a short note hits your phone. If it is not, a polite close and a callback request.

4

You confirm

Pricing, arrival windows, and anything that costs money still come from you. The desk does not freelance heroics.

What the desk will not do

This is the part where most “AI agents” get cute. We do not.

  • No taking payment
  • No contracts or surprise arrival times
  • No messages off-script
