How this ground behaves

  • Water. High water table. Wells can be shallow and unhappy. Surface water is not a domestic right you just take.
  • Septic. Mound and capping-fill country. Budget the high end or walk.
  • Access. County roads exist. Spring thaw eats driveways.
  • Zoning. Floodplain and wetland overlays eat building envelope.
  • Winter. Ice and a high water table. Not a basement plan.
