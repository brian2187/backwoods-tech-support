The well pump rides the outage. Heat is wood, propane, electric, or a combination. Off-grid is legal with the right system; it is not a bargain bin.

Answer these

Yes, no, or not sure. Not sure is a fair answer — it shows up at the end as something still to figure out.

  • Do you know which utility serves the place — Avista, Northern Lights, Inland, or none?

  • If there is no line, do you have a line-extension estimate in writing?

  • Do you have a generator or battery plan for the well pump and a freezer?

  • Heat: wood, propane, or both — with dry wood or a delivery account?
