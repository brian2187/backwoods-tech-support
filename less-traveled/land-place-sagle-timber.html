How this ground behaves

  • Water. Often no log on file, or a neighbor log. Plan $20–40k+ to drill. Storage if the well is shy.
  • Septic. Vacant timber is a PHD site eval during inspection, not a vibe. Mound money if the draw is wet.
  • Access. Private easements. Ask who plows. A gentleman’s agreement is not legal access.
  • Zoning. Often rural / AF. Extra dwelling is not automatic.
  • Winter. Shade holds snow. South openings matter for garden and solar.
