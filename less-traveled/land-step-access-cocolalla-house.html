Recorded access and a winter plow story matter as much as the house. A two-track in the photos is not enough.

What to know

  • Legal access is on the deed or title, not a two-track in the listing photos.
  • Who plows, with what, belongs in writing.
  • A new driveway often needs an approach permit from the highway district or ITD.
  • School buses do not always turn around where a listing implies.

Offices and records

  • Bonner County
  • Idaho 511 (winter roads)

Legal access is recorded. Maintenance of a private road is a separate contract. The county will not adopt your spur because you asked.
