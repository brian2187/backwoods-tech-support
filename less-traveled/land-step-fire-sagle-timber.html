This region burns. A timber lot without a break is winter wood and a July problem.

On this type. Defensible space is insurance, not aesthetics. BonFire fuels work is nearby.

What to know

  • Defensible space is ongoing, not a listing photo.
  • Ask a local agent if the address is insurable.
  • Burn restrictions and who issues a burn permit change with the season.
  • A timber cut needs access and a logger who will show up.
