About us

We live here. These pages are how we try to be useful.

We’re neighbors in Sagle. Less Traveled is the stuff people around here actually need: land and water, weather and roads, what’s on this weekend, who still picks up the phone when a well runs dry.

We care about this community, and we’re here to support it however we can. If a camera, a lake graph, or a straight answer on perc saves you a trip, that’s the work. If you see a hole in the board, or you need a hand finding a record, say so.

We keep a street address off the public web. Use the form below.
