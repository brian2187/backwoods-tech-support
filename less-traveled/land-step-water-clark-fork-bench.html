Rural Bonner is wells. The log and a pump test tell you whether a house and stock make it through August.

What to know

  • Pull an IDWR well log for the parcel. A listing that says the area is good is not a log.
  • Gallons per minute and storage matter. Four GPM can run a house; fifteen is comfort. Stock and irrigation want more.
  • A water test and a pump test during inspection are cheap compared to a dry August.
  • Hay or orchard irrigation needs a water right. A garden hose is not one.

Offices and records

  • IDWR well construction search
  • How to pull a well log

Domestic wells are regulated. Irrigation and stockwater can need a right. IDWR is the office. A neighbor’s ditch story is not.
