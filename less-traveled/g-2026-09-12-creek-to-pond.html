Creek to pond, still unfinished

Trench is in. Pond cleanup is not a brochure. No straw fill.

Sep 12, 2026

We ran water from the creek toward the pond. The trench is done. Pond cleanup is still the job. No straw in the trench — that idea did not survive contact with this ground.

This is the journal, not a how-to for your easement. If you are buying a place with a creek and a dream of a pond, start with setbacks, wetlands, and PHD, not a weekend of YouTube. The situation board will tell you if the river is high. The land guides will tell you who to call.

We will write the next one when there is something true to add.
