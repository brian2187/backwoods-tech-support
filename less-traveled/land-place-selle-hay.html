How this ground behaves

  • Water. Well on file more often. Ask GPM and a recent pump test. Irrigation is a water-right question, not a hose.
  • Septic. Existing system. Confirm it was finaled and sized for the bedrooms you think you bought.
  • Access. More county road than Sagle timber, still confirm plow route.
  • Zoning. AF lots show up here. Ag exemption is paperwork with the assessor, not a lifestyle.
  • Winter. Wind on open ground. Hay storage and a shop you can get a tractor into.
