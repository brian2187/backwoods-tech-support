Aimed at type Ponderay / city-edge shop lot. Clear type

Off city sewer, you are buying a septic system. Creek lots and high water tables can turn a gravity field into a mound.

What to know

  • Start with the parcel number from the assessor.
  • Panhandle Health keeps septic records by section.
  • A finaled permit and room for a replacement field are the deal, not a tank lid in the grass.
  • Drainfields want about 100 feet from wells, and setback from creek or lake.

Offices and records

  • PHD septic permits & records
  • How to pull a septic record
  • 2026 septic cost bands

Panhandle Health District issues the permit in this region (Sandpoint office 2101 W. Pine). They are not the city. New permit fee in the 2026 guidelines: $1,250.
