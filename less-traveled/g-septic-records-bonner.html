How to pull a Panhandle Health septic record in Bonner County

Sep 12, 2026

If the place is outside city sewer, you are buying a septic system whether the listing says so or not. Panhandle Health District (PHD) issues the permit. The Sandpoint office is 2101 W. Pine St. They are not the City of Sandpoint.

What you need

  1. The parcel number (assessor / GIS). Example shape: RP59N01W218540A.
  2. From that number, Township, Range, Section. After RP the next chunks are often Township, Range, Section (59N, 01W, 21 in the example). Confirm on the assessor map; do not guess.
  3. PHD public septic record search: panhandlehealthdistrict.org — septic permits & records.

Search the section, not just the one lot. Neighbor systems tell you if this ground takes a cheap gravity field or a mound.

What “good” looks like

  • A permit that was finaled, not just applied.
  • Tank size and drainfield type that match the house you think you are buying.
  • Room for a replacement drainfield. PHD cares about this. Listings do not.

2026 money (local contractor ranges, Sagle/Sandpoint)

System Typical install
Gravity $10,000–$18,000
Gravity to pump $13,000–$22,000
Capping fill $18,000–$30,000
Sand mound $25,000–$40,000

New subsurface sewage permit fee in the 2026 PHD guidelines: $1,250. Repair permit is less; speculative site eval exists if you are buying vacant land.

Setbacks people miss

Drainfield 100 ft from any well. 100–300 ft from streams, lakes, ponds depending on soil. 5 ft from property line. Wet or creek-side lots fail this quietly.

This is a walkthrough of public records, not a design. Hire a licensed installer and PHD for the site you actually buy.
