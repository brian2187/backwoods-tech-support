About

Written from Sagle. Not a second newspaper. Not Zillow.

Less Traveled is a North Idaho network: land-and-water guides, a situation board, a journal of local goings-on, and a trades list. We live here. Street address stays off the public web.

Virtual Assistant is the shop phone desk from the same house: a dedicated line that answers when you cannot, then puts the job on your calendar. Enroll on that page.

Unending (the game) is still the heavy ship. This is residual — useful pages, updated on a box in the county.

AI, when it lands, is SpaceXAI on the server, grounded in these pages. It will not invent a well log.

Corrections and notes go to the contact page.
