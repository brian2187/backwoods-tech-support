Outside municipal sewer you will install or inherit an on-site system. PHD (Sandpoint) permits it. A licensed installer builds it.

Install ranges quoted locally in 2026

These are contractor-reported bands for Sagle / Sandpoint, not a bid.

  • Standard gravity — good soils, gentle slope: $10k–$18k
  • Gravity to pump — drainfield uphill of the tank: $13k–$22k
  • Capping fill — shallow soil to a limiting layer: $18k–$30k
  • Sand mound — poor soils or high water: $25k–$40k

Creek, marsh, and spring-flood lots often need the expensive end even when the surface looks like lawn.

Fees (PHD 2026 guidelines)

  • New subsurface sewage permit: $1,250
  • Repair (failing system): $500
  • Expansion of drainfield >10%: $500
  • Tank / vault / pit privy: $300

Soil and setbacks drive the number more than the brand of tank. Precast concrete is common here because of frost heave.

Get two licensed bids and a PHD site evaluation on vacant land during the inspection period.
