Sandpoint’s season is short. Frost pockets in draws kill orchards. Hay wants open ground. A high tunnel is how a lot of kitchens make it.

Skip this page if you are not running garden, orchard, greenhouse, hay, cattle. The worksheet will hide it once your plan says so.

Answer these

Yes, no, or not sure. Not sure is a fair answer — it shows up at the end as something still to figure out.

  • Does the garden or hay site get winter sun, or is it a north timber wall?

  • Are you planning for late spring and early fall frost?

  • If you want hay or cattle, do you know what actually came off the field?

  • Garden water: does the well have the gallons, and a frost-free hydrant plan?
