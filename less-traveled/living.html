Relocator

Living here

Sandpoint is a resort town with rural infrastructure. Beautiful is not the same as easy.

City proper is about ten thousand people. Bonner County is wells, septic, wood, propane, and a lake that draws buyers faster than wages move.

July 2026 Selkirk Association snapshot: Bonner median sale around $650,000. Local household income sits well below what that mortgage wants. Remote salaries buy; many local jobs do not.

Winter in town is often 60–70 inches. Schweitzer is another climate. You need tires, a plow plan, and a story for when the well pump dies with the power.

Healthcare access, including labor and delivery, is thinner than a metro relocator expects. Check Bonner General and what is a drive to Spokane or CDA before you need it.
