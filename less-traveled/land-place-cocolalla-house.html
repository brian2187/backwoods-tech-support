How this ground behaves

  • Water. Log should exist. 4–8 GPM with a tank is common. Test for coliform during inspection.
  • Septic. Age the tank. Replacement field room. PHD record search before you waive inspection.
  • Access. Often on a named road. Still ask about the hill in January.
  • Zoning. Smaller lots. Extra dwelling is the fight.
  • Winter. Doable. Wood or propane backup when the well pump rides the outage.
