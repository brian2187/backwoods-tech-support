Living here

A resort town with rural infrastructure

Sandpoint proper is about ten thousand people. Bonner County is wells, septic, wood, propane, and a lake that a lot of people want to live on.

  • July 2026 Selkirk Association snapshot: Bonner median sale around $650,000. The listing price is not the whole bill — well, septic, and the first winter sit on top of it.
  • Winter in town is often 60–70 inches. Schweitzer is another climate. Tires, a plow plan, and a backup for the well pump are part of living here.
  • Healthcare, including labor and delivery, is thinner than a metro. Bonner General first; Spokane or Coeur d’Alene for a lot of specialty care.
