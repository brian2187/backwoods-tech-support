  • Civil outfits here also do perc holes, road layout, and septic design.

3 · What you may build

Zoning and building permits

R and AF lots around Sandpoint are not the same. Extra dwellings, shops, and a tiny home each have a path — or they do not.

  • Look up zoning on the county map, not the listing adjective.
  • A building permit is the county (or the city if you are inside one). Bring the parcel number to the counter.
  • Owner-builder has limits. Electrical, plumbing, septic, and well are licensed trades.

4 · How the parcel drinks

Water and well permits

Rural Bonner is wells. The log and a pump test tell you whether a house makes it through August.

  • Pull an IDWR well log for the parcel. A listing that says the area is good is not a log.
  • Four GPM can run a house; storage helps. Stock and irrigation want more, and irrigation is a water right.
  • A water test and a pump test during inspection are cheap compared to a dry well.

5 · What the ground will take

Septic and perc

Off city sewer, you are buying a septic system. Creek lots and a high water table can turn a gravity field into a mound.

  • Panhandle Health keeps septic records by section. Start with the parcel number from the assessor.
  • A finaled permit and room for a replacement field are the deal, not a tank lid in the grass.
  • Drainfields want about 100 feet from wells, and setback from creek or lake. New permit fee in the 2026 guidelines: $1,250.

Power to the property

  • Electrical work is licensed. Off-grid still meets code if you pull a building permit.

  • Rock, crush, and haul are their own bill on timber lots.

8 · New build, prefab, tiny, shop

House type

Stick-built, a manufactured or cabin package, a tiny home, or a post-frame shop with living space. Zoning decides which of those you can actually set.

  • New construction is a licensed general contractor and a building permit. Williams Homes has a community off Boyer; others build on your parcel.
  • Prefab, portable cedar cabins, and tiny homes still need a legal dwelling path, septic, and power. Ask Planning before you order the unit.
  • Post-frame and steel shops are common here. A 40x60 is a permit and a neighbor issue.

10 · When the line dies

Heat, wood, and propane

Heat here is wood, propane, electric, or a mix. The well pump and a freezer need a plan for the outage.

  • A propane tank set needs setbacks from the house and the property line. The fire district cares.
  • We do not have a dedicated propane dealer in the directory. Ask who delivers on your road, then confirm the tank set yourself.
  • Dry wood, or a delivery account. Town is often 60–70 inches. Schweitzer is another climate.

12 · How mail finds you

Mailbox and address

A rural parcel does not automatically get a mailbox. You need an address from the county, then a box the carrier will actually stop at.

  • Bonner County Planning assigns addresses. USPS decides the box style and location.
  • Cluster boxes are common on private spurs. A roadside box needs a pull-out the plow will not bury.
  • The UPS Store in Sandpoint rents boxes if the carrier will not come up your road yet.
