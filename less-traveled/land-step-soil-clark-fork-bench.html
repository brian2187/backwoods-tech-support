Sandpoint’s season is short. Frost pockets in draws are hard on orchards. Hay wants open ground.

Most useful if you care about garden, orchard, greenhouse, hay, cattle.

What to know

  • Garden and hay want winter sun, not a north timber wall.
  • Late spring and early fall frost are normal here.
  • If you want hay or cattle, ask what actually came off the field.
  • Garden water needs well gallons and a frost-free hydrant plan.

Offices and records

  • NRCS Web Soil Survey

Selling produce has cottage-food and scale rules. A kitchen garden for the house does not. Irrigation water is still IDWR.
