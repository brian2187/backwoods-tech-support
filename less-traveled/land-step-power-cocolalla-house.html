The well pump rides the outage. Heat is wood, propane, electric, or a mix. Off-grid is legal with the right system.

What to know

  • Confirm which utility serves the place: Avista, Northern Lights, Inland Power, or none.
  • No line means a line-extension estimate in writing before you close.
  • A generator or battery plan for the well pump and a freezer is local common sense.
  • Heat is wood, propane, or both — with dry wood or a delivery account.

Offices and records

  • Avista

Electrical work is licensed. Off-grid still meets code if you pull a building permit. Utility easements are on the title work.
